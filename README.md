# Groundline

**From what you see to what gets done — with your approval.**

Point a camera at a label or document, speak your goal, and Groundline grounds the
exact region on screen, drafts the matching web form in a sandboxed browser, and
waits for your approval before anything is submitted.

Nothing is ever submitted, purchased or sent without an explicit human decision.

## Status

Day 1 of 10. Under active construction during the event window.

## Stack

- Frontend: Next.js (App Router), camera capture and mask overlay
- Backend: FastAPI, holds every credential
- Models: Muse Spark and SAM 3.1 via Meta Model API; Muse Spark Open for routing
- Browser agent: Playwright in an isolated sandbox, allowlisted domains only
